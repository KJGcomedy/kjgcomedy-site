Q&A

Questions people ask before they laugh.

Can comedy work for my product?

Almost always. I've made funny ads for restaurant software, personal care products, apps and online stores. If it has a customer with a problem, there's a joke in it.

Do I need a script?

No. Send yours, or add Written by KJG and I'll write it. Not sure where to start? The Pitch Meeting gets you three concepts for $95.

Can I run it as a paid ad?

Yes. Every video includes organic social use. Paid-ad usage is an add-on, starting at +30% for 3 months.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the script and the shoot. You get a firm delivery date before any work starts. Have a deadline? Tell me on the call and I'll say honestly whether I can hit it.

Do you post the videos?

No. I play a character, your customer, your expert or your CEO, and you post the video on your own channels.

Can my likeness or voice be used with AI?

No. My contract rules out using my likeness or voice to train or generate AI content, and keeps my real name off your ads. Trust me, you want it that way.

Can kids appear in the video?

I'm happy to play a parent, and a kid. No real children on camera.

How do I pay?

By card at checkout. Larger projects and series are 50% up front, with the balance due before final files.

Do you make straight, non-comedy videos?

Yes: UGC-style ads and spokesperson videos. See my Full UGC Portfolio - Full Spokesperson Portfolio, or book a call.
